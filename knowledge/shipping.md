# Shipping Policy

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within India. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs ₹99.

## Delayed orders
If an order has not arrived within 7 business days, the customer can request a full refund or a free reshipment. Delays must be reported within 30 days of the order date.

## Tracking
A tracking link is emailed when the order ships. Customers can also track orders in the NovaCart app under My Orders.
